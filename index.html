How to play

A compact ludo on a 5×5 circuit: an outer lap, a guarded inner ring, and the crown in the middle.

The track

  • Every token starts in its yard and enters the board on its own coloured block.
  • The outer ring (16 squares) runs clockwise. The inner ring (8 squares) runs anti-clockwise. The centre is home.
  • The coloured square on the inner ring facing your side of the board is the one you step into the centre from.
  • The four coloured blocks are safe squares — nobody can be cut while standing on one.

Getting in

  • Roll a 6 (or a 1, if enabled) to bring a token out of the yard. A 6 also earns a bonus roll.
  • Three sixes in a row forfeits the turn.

Cutting

  • Land on a lone opponent outside a safe square and it is cut — straight back to its yard. You get a bonus roll.
  • Two or more of your tokens on one square form a blockade: opponents cannot land on it, though they may pass.
  • Cutting works in the inner ring too.

Into the inner ring

  • A token may only turn inwards after it has completed a full lap and has cut an opponent at least once.
  • A token that has earned its cut carries a white star. Without the star it simply laps again.
  • The turn-in happens automatically, from the square just before your own block.

Home

  • Follow the inner ring all the way round to the square facing the centre, then step in with an exact roll.
  • Reaching home earns a bonus roll. First player to bring the required number of tokens home wins.
